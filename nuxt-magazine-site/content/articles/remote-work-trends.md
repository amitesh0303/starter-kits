## The Shift to Remote

The pandemic accelerated a trend that was already underway. Now, three years later, remote work has become a permanent fixture in many industries.

## Key Statistics

- 65% of workers prefer hybrid arrangements
- Productivity has increased 13% for remote workers
- Companies save an average of $11,000 per remote worker annually

## Best Practices

### Communication

Asynchronous communication tools have become essential for distributed teams.

### Culture Building

Virtual team events and regular check-ins help maintain connection.
