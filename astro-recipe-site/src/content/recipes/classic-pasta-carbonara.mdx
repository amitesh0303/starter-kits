## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook pasta until al dente.
2. While pasta cooks, cook guanciale in a large skillet over medium heat until crispy.
3. In a bowl, whisk together egg yolks, whole eggs, and most of the pecorino.
4. Drain pasta, reserving 1 cup of pasta water.
5. Toss hot pasta with guanciale, then remove from heat and add egg mixture.
6. Toss vigorously, adding pasta water as needed to create a creamy sauce.
7. Serve immediately with remaining pecorino and black pepper.
