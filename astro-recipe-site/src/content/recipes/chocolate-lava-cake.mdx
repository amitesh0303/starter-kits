  - "75g caster sugar"
  - "50g plain flour"
  - "Butter and cocoa powder for ramekins"

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 220C (425F). Butter four ramekins and dust with cocoa powder.
2. Melt chocolate and butter together in a double boiler until smooth.
3. Whisk eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until thick and pale.
4. Fold melted chocolate into the egg mixture.
5. Sift in flour and fold gently until just combined.
6. Divide batter between ramekins. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
7. Bake for 12-14 minutes until edges are set but center is still jiggly.
8. Let rest for 1 minute, then invert onto plates and serve immediately.
